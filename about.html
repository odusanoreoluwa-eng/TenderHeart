About Tender Heart

We are particularly proud of our specialized expertise in dementia care and palliative support, areas where we provide enhanced compassion and professional understanding. Beyond our robust in-home services, we also operate our own dedicated care facility.

Our Story

Established over 10 years ago, Tender Heart Care Solution has grown into a trusted name in elder care across Nigeria. Our extensive experience has equipped us with a deep understanding of the intricacies of aged care, allowing us to develop comprehensive and flexible care plans tailored to each individual's specific requirements.

Tender Heart was created to address a simple need: elder care that feels human—consistent, respectful, and truly supportive. We believe families deserve peace of mind and elders deserve dignity in every interaction.

  • Human-centered care
  • Family-first support

We provide reliable, compassionate care, allowing families to rest assured their loved ones are in good hands.

  • Respect & dignity
  • Peace of Mind for Families
